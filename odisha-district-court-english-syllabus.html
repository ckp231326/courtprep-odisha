Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist preparation

Odisha District Court English Syllabus and Preparation Guide

The English paper is descriptive. This guide explains the five components and their marks in recent 2026 Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist recruitment advertisements, and maps them to the English study resources available from CourtPrep Odisha.

COURTPREP ODISHA · Reviewed 16 November 2026

English paper: five components and 100 marks

The 2026 Nuapada and Ganjam advertisements divide the English paper into the following components:

Paper componentMarks
Essay writing in English30
Letter or application writing in English20
Odia passage translated into English15
English passage translated into Odia15
Summary of an English passage20
Total100
The notices state that the required standard is equal to that of a +3 examination conducted by a recognised university. Check the latest advertisement for the judgeship where you apply.

Prepare each component as a separate skill

The paper tests different forms of written expression. Reading model answers can help, but candidates also need to produce their own work under time limits.

  • Essay: practise understanding the topic, planning a clear structure, developing relevant paragraphs and writing a conclusion.
  • Letter or application: learn the appropriate format, purpose, tone and sequence of information. Practise both formal and informal letter tasks where relevant to your preparation.
  • Odia to English translation: focus on conveying the meaning in natural English, sentence restructuring, tense, auxiliaries and contextual vocabulary.
  • English to Odia translation: practise natural Odia sentence construction and accurate transfer of meaning rather than translating word by word.
  • Summary: identify the central idea, retain essential points, remove repetition and stay within the required word limit or instruction.

Which CourtPrep Odisha volumes support the English paper?

The English collection is divided into six volumes, each focused on a particular task or question archive.

  • English Volume I: collected previous-year English papers from 2016–2025.
  • English Volume II: essay writing, previous-year topic analysis, master essays and an idea bank.
  • English Volume III: formal and informal letters and application writing.
  • English Volume IV: summary and précis writing methods, compression techniques and solved practice.
  • English Volume V: Odia-to-English translation, grammar, sentence restructuring and bilingual explanations.
  • English Volume VI: English-to-Odia translation, sentence structure and contextual vocabulary.

How to practise the 100-mark paper

A practical revision method is to rotate the five components across your study sessions and periodically attempt a complete paper under examination conditions. After each attempt, review structure, accuracy, grammar, meaning, clarity and time management. These are study suggestions, not an official scoring rubric.

Frequently asked questions

How many marks is the English paper?

The cited 2026 notices show a 100-mark English paper.

How many marks are allotted to essay writing?

Essay writing carries 30 marks in the cited notices.

Are both translation directions included?

Yes. The listed components include one Odia passage translated into English and one English passage translated into Odia, each carrying 15 marks.

Which book covers summary writing?

English Volume IV is the focused summary and précis writing guide.

Does CourtPrep Odisha provide previous-year English papers?

Yes. English Volume I contains collected English question papers from 2016–2025.
