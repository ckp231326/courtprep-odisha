Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist preparation

Odisha District Court Arithmetic Syllabus and Preparation Guide

A topic-wise guide to the Arithmetic portion of the Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist written examination, based on the subject list in 2026 District Court recruitment advertisements.

COURTPREP ODISHA · Reviewed 16 November 2026

Arithmetic topics mentioned in the 2026 notices

The Nuapada Advertisement No. 01 of 2026 and Ganjam Advertisement No. 02/Red./2026 list the following areas for Arithmetic:

  • Vulgar fractions and decimals
  • H.C.F. and L.C.M.
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Percentages, profit and loss
  • Mixtures, partnership and averages
  • Rates and taxes, and insurance
  • Square and cubic measures
  • Problems on time and work, and time and distance
The notices also state that problems more easily solvable by algebraic methods need not be required to be solved arithmetically. Read the exact advertisement for the wording applicable to your recruitment.

How to organise the topics for study

The following is a practical grouping for revision, not an official division of the syllabus.

  • Number foundation: fractions, decimals, HCF and LCM
  • Commercial arithmetic: percentages, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, mixtures, partnership and averages
  • Measures and applications: rates and taxes, insurance, square and cubic measures
  • Work and movement: time and work, time and distance

Use concepts and previous-year questions together

Start with the method and formula, solve a few examples without looking at the solution, and then practise a set of previous-year questions. Record errors by topic so you can return to the specific concept that caused difficulty.

  • Arithmetic Volume II – Zero to Hero is the concept-focused guide with formulas, worked examples, descriptive solutions and practice exercises.
  • Arithmetic Volume I contains collected previous-year question papers from 2021–2025, organised by examination year and judgeship.
  • Compare the official topic list with the detailed contents of the volumes. In particular, check the treatment of rates and taxes and insurance rather than assuming every listed topic is fully covered.

A simple practice cycle

For each topic, use a repeatable sequence: understand the concept, learn the formula, solve examples, attempt questions without assistance, check the solution and revise mistakes. Once you can solve a topic accurately, practise mixed questions under a time limit.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the official Arithmetic topic list?

The cited 2026 notices list fractions and decimals, HCF and LCM, simple and compound interest, percentages, profit and loss, mixtures, partnership, averages, rates and taxes, insurance, square and cubic measures, time and work, and time and distance.

Which CourtPrep Odisha book contains previous-year Arithmetic questions?

Arithmetic Volume I contains collected question papers from 2021–2025, arranged year-wise and judgeship-wise.

Which book is intended for learning Arithmetic concepts?

Arithmetic Volume II – Zero to Hero is the concept-focused guide. Check its detailed contents and sample pages to identify the topics you need.

Does the book collection cover the Computer Science practical test?

No. The Arithmetic books support the Arithmetic subject. The computer practical test is a separate part of the selection process and needs separate practice.
