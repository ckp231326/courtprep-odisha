Privacy Policy

Customer data and privacy notice · Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026
This notice explains what information is collected when you browse the catalogue, place an order, request a free resource or contact support.

1. Who is responsible?

COURTPREP ODISHA is an independent educational digital-resource service owned and operated by Rasmita Panda, Proprietor, at 119 Colony, Malkangiri, Odisha – 764048, India. For privacy questions, email ckpbpo@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

  • Customer details: name, Gmail address and mobile number supplied during order or free-resource registration.
  • Order details: selected e-books/packages, order reference, date/time, amount, order and delivery status.
  • Payment-reference details: UPI transaction reference/UTR entered by you and payment-verification outcome. We do not ask for or collect your UPI PIN, bank password, card PIN or banking OTP.
  • Verification and service records: email OTP verification status, delivery/access records, emails sent and relevant support correspondence.
  • Technical information: limited technical logs generated by the hosting and Google services used to operate the website and checkout.

3. Purposes of use

  • verify the Gmail address and prevent incorrect delivery;
  • create and manage orders and free-resource registrations;
  • verify receipt of payment before approving a paid order;
  • share the selected PDF with the verified Gmail address;
  • send order, payment, delivery and support communications;
  • prevent duplicate claims, fraud, misuse and unauthorised access; and
  • maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use Google Apps Script, Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Drive for verification, order records, communication and PDF delivery. Public website files and catalogue assets are hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices. Your UPI app or payment service processes payment separately. We do not receive your UPI PIN or bank login credentials.

We do not sell customer personal information. We do not publish customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, UTRs or order details on the public website.

Advertising measurement: Our public website loads the Google tag associated with our Google Ads account (tag ID: GTM-MFTTS7BB) to support advertising measurement. Depending on the tag configuration and applicable settings, Google may receive technical information about page visits and interactions, such as the page address, referring page and browser or device information. Google technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers where enabled and permitted by applicable settings and requirements. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. PDF access and technical limits

The relevant PDF is shared with the Gmail address you verify. Download, print and copy controls may be restricted where Google Drive supports them. These controls cannot guarantee prevention of screenshots, screen recording, photographing a screen or every form of copying.

6. Storage and security

Order and payment-reference records are held in the private store records used to operate the service. Verification and delivery information may also be held by the Google services used for the transaction. We use access controls and limit access to operational purposes. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil orders, maintain proof of payment and delivery, handle grievances, prevent fraud and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. Information may be retained longer where a dispute, legal obligation or lawful request requires it.

8. Your requests

You may request correction of inaccurate details, ask about information associated with your order, or request deletion where we are not required to retain it. Write to ckpbpo@gmail.com and include your order reference. We may verify your identity. Removing Drive access may affect your ability to use purchased material. Records required for accounting, disputes or legal compliance may be retained.

9. Children

The catalogue is intended for examination aspirants and is not directed at children. A person who is not legally competent to contract should use the service through a parent or lawful guardian.

10. Updates

We may update this notice when the website, service providers or applicable law changes. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page.
