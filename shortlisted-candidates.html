How to check a shortlist or recruitment notice

Use the original official publication before relying on any update.

1. Open the official website

Select your district from the directory above. Confirm that the address bar shows the relevant official court website.

2. Check Recruitment / Notices

Look for the recruitment, latest news, notices or announcements section. Open the original PDF issued by that District Court.

3. Verify date and instructions

Check the post, examination stage, publication date, venue, reporting time and any corrigendum or later notice.
