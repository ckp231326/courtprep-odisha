Refund & Cancellation Policy

Digital product refunds, delivery issues and duplicate payments · Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026
Our approach: If payment or delivery goes wrong, contact us promptly so we can check the order and resolve the issue.

1. Change of mind after access

Because an e-book is a digital product that may be accessed immediately after delivery, a refund is generally not available solely because a customer changes their mind, no longer needs the material or decides not to use it after access has been granted.

2. Duplicate payment

If the same order is paid for more than once and the duplicate amount is actually received, the duplicate payment will be refunded after verification. Provide the order reference and transaction references for both payments.

3. Payment received but material not delivered

If payment has been received but the purchased PDF has not been delivered, contact us. We will first attempt delivery to the correct verified Gmail address. If delivery cannot reasonably be completed, a refund will be offered for the affected order.

4. Incorrect, defective or materially incomplete file

If the file is corrupt, cannot be opened due to a file defect, is materially different from the product description or is materially incomplete, contact us with details. Depending on the issue, we will provide a corrected/replacement file or offer a refund where the problem cannot be appropriately resolved.

5. Technical access problems

If you cannot open a PDF or access the Drive file, contact support first. We may check the Gmail address, sharing permission, browser compatibility and Google service availability. If access remains unavailable and delivery cannot be completed, section 3 applies.

6. How to request a refund or correction

Email ckpbpo@gmail.com with your name, order ID, verified Gmail address, affected product/package, payment UTR/reference and date (if applicable), and a short description. Attach a relevant screenshot if useful, hiding unrelated personal or banking information.

7. Verification and processing

Refund requests are considered after checking the order, payment credit and delivery status. Where approved, the refund will ordinarily be sent through the original payment route or another mutually suitable traceable method. Bank, UPI app and payment-provider processing times may vary. We will communicate the outcome and any further information required.

8. Free resources

Free resources do not involve a purchase price and are not eligible for a price refund. If a free resource is not delivered or cannot be accessed, contact support and we will try to correct the access or delivery issue.

9. Statutory rights preserved

This policy does not limit any remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer-protection law. Where law requires a refund, replacement, repair or another remedy, that law prevails over any inconsistent wording in this policy.
