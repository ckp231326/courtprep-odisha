Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist preparation

Odisha District Court General Knowledge Syllabus and Preparation Guide

The General Knowledge description in recent Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist advertisements is broad rather than a fixed list of chapters. This guide explains the official wording and shows how to organise revision without confusing a book's chapter list with the official syllabus.

COURTPREP ODISHA · Reviewed 16 November 2026

What the official notice says about General Knowledge

The cited 2026 Nuapada and Ganjam advertisements describe General Knowledge as knowledge of current events and such other matters of everyday observation and experience as may be expected from an educated person.

The cited notices do not set out a detailed chapter-by-chapter General Knowledge list in this section. Therefore, the topic groups below are useful revision categories and the coverage of the CourtPrep Odisha reference book, not a claim that every heading is a separately prescribed official syllabus topic.

Organise your GK revision into useful areas

A broad general-awareness preparation plan can group information into subjects that are easier to revise and revisit.

  • Indian Constitution and Polity, and Judiciary and Legal General Knowledge
  • Odisha History, Geography, Culture, Literature, Personalities, Districts and Institutions
  • Indian History and Indian and World Geography
  • General Science, Space, Technology, Computer and Digital Awareness
  • Economy and Banking
  • Books, Authors, Art and Culture; Awards, Honours, Sports and Important Days
  • International Organisations, World GK, Abbreviations and related factual topics
  • Current affairs across Odisha, national and international developments

What is included in the CourtPrep Odisha GK reference volume?

GK Volume I is organised into twenty subject chapters. Its catalogue description lists Constitution and Polity, Judiciary and Legal GK, Odisha-related areas, Indian History and Geography, Science and Technology, Computer Awareness, Economy, Books and Authors, Awards, Sports, Important Days, international organisations and current-affairs categories.

  • Use the detailed contents and sample pages to see the specific coverage before purchase.
  • The book's topic organisation is the publisher's study structure; it should not be represented as an official chapter-wise syllabus.
  • For current affairs, check the publication's stated coverage period in the actual book before relying on it for a particular examination date.

Use previous-year questions to guide revision

GK Volume II contains collected Odisha District Court General Knowledge questions from 2016–2025, arranged by examination year and judgeship, with solved answers and answer-key support. Attempt questions first, check the answers afterwards and note the subjects where you repeatedly make mistakes.

A consistent GK revision routine

Set aside regular time for current events and general awareness, revise static facts in small groups, and solve previous-year questions at intervals. Keep separate notes for topics you confuse and revisit them instead of repeatedly reading only familiar chapters.

Frequently asked questions

Does the official General Knowledge syllabus give a fixed chapter list?

The cited 2026 advertisements describe General Knowledge broadly as current events and matters of everyday observation and experience expected from an educated person. They do not provide a detailed chapter-by-chapter list in this section.

Which CourtPrep Odisha book contains GK reference chapters?

GK Volume I is the organised General Knowledge and Current Affairs reference volume.

Are previous-year GK questions available?

Yes. GK Volume II contains collected previous-year questions from 2016–2025, organised year-wise and judgeship-wise, with solved answers and answer-key support.

Are the publisher's chapters the official syllabus?

No. The chapter list describes the contents of the study book. Candidates should compare it with the latest official recruitment notice and use it as a study aid.
