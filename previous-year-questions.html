How to use previous-year question papers

Start by attempting a paper or question set without referring to the answer. Note the topics and question styles that require further practice. Then use the subject-specific guide to strengthen the concepts or writing skills revealed by your practice.

  • Use Arithmetic Volume I to practise collected questions, then use Arithmetic Volume II for concepts, methods and worked examples.
  • Use GK Volume II to practise collected questions and check the provided solved answers and answer-key support.
  • Use English Volume I to understand the descriptive paper pattern, then consult the focused English volumes for essay, letter, application, summary and translation practice.

Frequently asked questions

Which examination years are covered?

Arithmetic Volume I covers collected question papers from 2021–2025. General Knowledge Volume II and English Volume I cover collected question papers from 2016–2025.

Are the question papers arranged by judgeship?

Yes. The three volumes describe year-wise and judgeship-wise organisation of the collected question sets.

Do all three books provide solved answers?

The General Knowledge previous-year volume specifically includes solved answers and answer-key support. Check the individual book details for the contents and features of the Arithmetic and English volumes.

Can I preview the books before purchasing?

Sample pages are available for the three listed books. Open the sample preview link in the relevant product card before placing an order.
