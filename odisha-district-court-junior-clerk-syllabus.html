Odisha District Court Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist Syllabus 2026 and Exam Pattern

Understand the written examination, subject-wise marks, English question components, Arithmetic topics, General Knowledge and Computer Science practical test using recent official District Court recruitment advertisements.

Prepared by COURTPREP ODISHA · Reviewed 16 November 2026

Important: check the advertisement for your judgeship

This guide summarises details published in two 2026 recruitment advertisements: Nuapada Advertisement No. 01 of 2026 dated 15 May 2026, and Ganjam Advertisement No. 02/Red./2026 dated 18 June 2026. Both refer to the Odisha District & Civil Courts' Judicial Staff Services Rules, 2008, as amended up to 2026.

Use this as a study reference, not as a replacement for the advertisement issued by the District Judge for the judgeship where you apply. Always verify the latest notice, amendment, dates, eligibility conditions and selection procedure from the relevant official district court website.

Independent study resource: COURTPREP ODISHA is not an official District Court publication or recruitment authority. The e-books linked on this page support selected written subjects; they do not claim to cover every part of the selection process.

Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist examination pattern

The 2026 Nuapada and Ganjam advertisements describe the following broad scheme for Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist:

Stage / SubjectMarksDuration
Written examination – English1002 hours
Written examination – Arithmetic1001 hour
Written examination – General Knowledge1001 hour
Computer Science Test (Practical)1001 hour
Viva-voce Test45As notified

The Nuapada advertisement explicitly lists 45 marks for viva-voce. It states that candidates securing 35% marks in each written paper are called for the Computer Science practical test in the prescribed ratio, with 50% as the qualifying mark for general candidates and 40% for SC/ST candidates. Read the relevant advertisement for the category-specific conditions applicable to your recruitment.

English syllabus and marks distribution

The 2026 notices divide the 100-mark English paper into five descriptive tasks:

English componentMarks
Essay writing in English30
Letter or application writing in English20
Translation of one Odia passage into English15
Translation of one English passage into Odia15
Summary of one English passage20
Total100

The Ganjam and Nuapada notices state that the required standard is equivalent to the +3 examination conducted by a recognised university. This makes regular writing and translation practice important; reading grammar rules alone is not a substitute for timed descriptive practice.

Arithmetic syllabus

The official topic list in the 2026 notices includes:

  • Vulgar fractions and decimals
  • H.C.F. and L.C.M.
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Percentages, profit and loss
  • Mixtures, partnership and averages
  • Rates and taxes, and insurance
  • Square and cubic measures
  • Problems on time and work, and time and distance

The notice also says that problems more easily solved by algebraic methods need not be required to be solved arithmetically. Refer to the notice itself for the precise wording.

The CourtPrep Odisha Arithmetic collection includes a previous-year question volume and a step-by-step concepts and practice volume. Compare their individual contents with the official topic list and supplement any topic not sufficiently covered.

General Knowledge syllabus

The official description is broad: knowledge of current events and matters of everyday observation and experience expected from an educated person. The notices do not prescribe a detailed chapter-by-chapter GK list in this section.

For preparation, candidates may organise revision across current affairs and general awareness themes. The CourtPrep Odisha GK volumes include an organised GK and Current Affairs reference, alongside collected previous-year GK questions from 2016–2025.

Computer Science Test (Practical): a separate preparation area

The 2026 notices describe practical proficiency in tasks such as:

  • Text formatting of paragraphs and insertion of tables
  • Printing and saving documents
  • File transfer
  • Website searching and browsing
  • Downloading and using e-mail
  • Using a pen drive and other software
  • Programmes of accounting

Important: The ten e-books currently listed by COURTPREP ODISHA cover Arithmetic, General Knowledge and English. They are not a Computer Science practical-test course. Candidates should make separate arrangements to practise the computer tasks listed in their recruitment notice.

Viva-voce test

The notices describe viva-voce as an assessment of a candidate's suitability, with particular reference to alertness, general outlook and potential qualities. Follow the instructions and criteria in the relevant recruitment notice.

A practical way to organise your preparation

  • Begin with the actual syllabus: make a checklist from the latest notice issued by your judgeship. Do not rely on an old advertisement alone.
  • Practise English as a writing paper: rotate essays, letters/applications, both translation directions and summaries. Use the published marks distribution to plan timed practice.
  • Build Arithmetic topic by topic: learn the method, solve worked examples and then attempt previous-year questions without looking at the solution.
  • Revise GK continuously: combine current events and broad general awareness with regular previous-year question practice.
  • Do not neglect the practical test: reserve separate time for document formatting, tables, printing, file operations, browsing, e-mail and other tasks specified in the notice.

These are general study suggestions, not an official timetable or a prediction of the questions that will appear.

Frequently asked questions

What are the written subjects for Junior Clerk-cum-Copyist?

The cited 2026 notices list English, Arithmetic and General Knowledge as the written subjects, followed by a Computer Science Test (Practical) and viva-voce.

How many marks is the English paper?

The cited notices show 100 marks: essay 30, letter or application 20, Odia-to-English translation 15, English-to-Odia translation 15 and summary 20.

Is a Computer Science practical test included?

Yes. The cited 2026 advertisements describe a separate 100-mark, one-hour Computer Science practical test. Check the relevant district advertisement for the applicable qualifying conditions.

Do the CourtPrep Odisha e-books cover the computer practical test?

No. The ten currently listed e-books cover Arithmetic, General Knowledge and English. Computer practical preparation must be done separately.

Does every District Court advertisement have exactly the same dates and conditions?

Do not assume that. Use the latest advertisement for the relevant judgeship to confirm dates, eligibility, category conditions, selection procedure and any specific instructions.
