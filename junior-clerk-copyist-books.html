Frequently asked questions

Are these official District Court publications?

No. CourtPrep Odisha is an independent educational initiative. These e-books are not official publications and are not affiliated with any District Court or recruitment authority.

Do these e-books cover the entire official Junior Clerk and Copyist syllabus?

This collection contains study resources in Arithmetic, General Knowledge and English, as described on this page and in each book's contents. It should not be treated as a confirmation that every requirement in a particular recruitment notification or official syllabus is covered. Always compare your preparation with the latest official notification and syllabus.

Can I check the contents before buying?

Yes. Each book card provides details and, where available, a sample-pages link. Review those materials before placing an order.

Are all books available in Odia?

No. Language differs by volume. The language information is displayed in each book's details; selected Arithmetic, English-writing and translation books include Odia guidance or bilingual explanations.

How do I receive a purchased e-book?

Enter and verify the Gmail address where you want access. After you make payment and the payment is checked and approved, access is granted to that verified Gmail address as described in the purchase process.
