Choose the issue you are facing

1. Payment deducted, but order is not confirmed

Send your order ID, payment date, amount and UPI transaction reference/UTR. We will check the payment record and actual credit before confirming the order.

2. Gmail verification code (OTP) not received

Check the spelling of your Gmail address, Spam/Junk folder and wait briefly before requesting another code. Contact us with your order ID and registered Gmail address if the problem continues.

3. Payment submitted, but PDF not delivered

Send your order ID, verified Gmail address and payment UTR/reference. We will check payment approval and delivery status.

4. Google Drive says you do not have access

Make sure you are signed in to the same Gmail address that you verified during checkout. Send your order ID and verified Gmail address if access still fails.

5. Incorrect Gmail address entered

Contact us promptly with your order ID and the correct Gmail address. We will verify the request before changing access details.

6. Duplicate payment or refund request

Send your order ID, the transaction references and a brief explanation. Do not send passwords, PINs or banking OTPs.

7. Checkout opens a Google Drive error page on Android

This can happen when Android routes an Apps Script checkout link through the Google Drive app, or when several Google Accounts are signed in to the same browser session. It does not by itself mean that a payment has been made or failed.

Try this: copy the checkout URL, open Chrome, choose New Incognito Tab, paste the URL and press Enter. Alternatively, sign out of additional Google Accounts and use one account in normal Chrome. If the page still does not open, contact support before making or repeating a payment.

Information to include in your message

  • Order ID (where an order has been created)
  • Your name
  • Gmail address used and verified during checkout
  • Issue category and a short description
  • Payment UTR/reference only when the issue concerns payment
Security warning: Never share your UPI PIN, ATM/card PIN, bank password, net-banking credentials or any OTP used to authorise a payment. COURTPREP ODISHA support will never ask for these details. Share only the transaction reference/UTR when payment verification is needed.
