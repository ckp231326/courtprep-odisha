General Knowledge and Current Affairs coverage

The reference volume brings together core static-GK subjects and current-affairs areas in one organised resource.

  • Indian Constitution and Polity; Judiciary and Legal General Knowledge
  • Odisha History, Geography, Culture, Literature, Personalities, Districts and Institutions
  • Indian History and Indian and World Geography
  • General Science, Space, Technology, Computer and Digital Awareness
  • Economy and Banking; Books, Authors, Art and Culture
  • Awards, Honours, Sports, Important Days and Dates
  • International Organisations, World GK, Abbreviations, Superlatives, Firsts and Sobriquets
  • Odisha, national and international current affairs, including government schemes, legal reforms, economy, science, defence, appointments, awards and sports

Understand the examination pattern through previous-year questions

The previous-year volume contains collected GK papers from 2016–2025. Papers are arranged by examination year and judgeship, with solved answers and answer-key support for practice and revision.

Frequently asked questions

Does the GK collection include previous-year questions?

Yes. General Knowledge Volume II contains collected Odisha District Court GK questions from 2016–2025, with year-wise and judgeship-wise organisation and solved-answer support.

Does the reference book cover Odisha-specific topics?

Yes. It includes Odisha History, Geography, Culture, Literature, Personalities, Districts, Places, Institutions and Odisha Current Affairs.

What is the language of the GK books?

The two General Knowledge volumes are in English.
